## PRICING STRATEGY

- **Start lower** to get first 3–5 reviews: Basic at $29–39
- **Raise prices** after 5★ reviews by $20–30 per package
- **Never compete on price alone** — add speed and clarity as value

## RED FLAGS — Orders to avoid
- "Can you scrape [social media requiring login]" → decline
- "I need this in 1 hour" on complex tasks → decline or charge rush fee (+50%)
- No clear description of what they need → ask before accepting

## PLATFORMS TO START

1. **Fiverr** — create gigs #1 and #2 first (scraping + automation)
   - Post gigs, wait for impressions, optimize tags
   - First orders may come in 2–4 weeks

2. **Guru.com** — post same services as fixed-price "services"
   - Also browse job board and send proposals
   - No AI policy = zero risk

3. **Later: Upwork** — higher earning potential, harder to start without reviews
   - Use Fiverr reviews as social proof in profile bio
```
